Privacy Policy 🕵️‍♂️

Developer: Oleksii Demchenko

Last Updated: November 7, 2026

Welcome to CRINGEorBASED. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data in line with App Store guidelines.

1. Data We Collect (Anonymous User IDs)

We do not collect personal information like your name, email, or contacts. We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication which creates a unique, anonymous User ID (UID). We do not track your hardware Device ID. This UID is stored securely in our database solely for:

  • Enforcing daily generation limits.
  • Tracking terms violations (NSFW/Violence attempts).
  • Permanently banning abusive UIDs to maintain a safe environment.
  • Crediting rewarded ads and running friend challenges and Daily Lore (see section 6).
  • Backing up your cards, packs and streak.
  • Sending notifications, if you allow them.

Your story ideas (text prompts) are stored on our servers together with the generated story (see section 7). Please do not include personal information in them.

Progress backup: your card collection, packs and Daily Lore streak are saved on our servers, linked to your UID. A recovery code lets you move them to another device. We store only a hash of the code, it works once, and anyone who has the code can claim that progress, so keep it private.

Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store a push token and your language, linked to your UID. Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver the notifications.

Stored only on your device: your language and app settings.

2. Third-Party Services

To provide our core features, we share strictly necessary data with trusted third-party APIs:

  • OpenAI: Your text prompts are sent to OpenAI for moderation and story generation. OpenAI does not use this data to train public models.
  • Klipy: We use Klipy's API to fetch relevant GIFs based on generated keywords.
  • Cloudinary: Delivers story images in older app versions. The current version does not use it.
  • Google Firebase: Used for anonymous sign-in (Authentication), protecting our servers from unauthorized apps (App Check), secure backend infrastructure (Firestore and Cloud Functions), delivering story images, card art and challenge links (Hosting), notifications (Cloud Messaging), crash reporting (Crashlytics), app usage tracking (Analytics), and dynamic app configuration (Remote Config).

3. Advertising & Analytics

Advertising: Our app offers rewarded ad viewing in exchange for in-game attempts. We use third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob). These networks may collect and process data like your IP address and device Advertising ID to serve relevant ads. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, you can manage or withdraw your consent for personalized ads at any time by tapping "Privacy" in the Menu (☰) on the Home screen; older app versions show this button at the bottom of the Home screen. Other users can opt-out of personalized ads via their device settings.

Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the app (e.g., screen views, error rates, finished stories, brain age results, shared cards, opened packs and challenges) to improve performance and user experience. We do not use this data to identify you personally. For more information on how Google uses your data, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms site.

4. Regional Privacy Compliance (GDPR, CCPA & ATT)

We respect global privacy standards:

  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS devices, we strictly follow Apple’s ATT framework. We will only access your Advertising Identifier (IDFA) if you explicitly grant permission via the system prompt.
  • GDPR (EEA & UK): We provide a Consent Management Platform. You can manage or withdraw your consent for personalized ads at any time via "Privacy" in the Menu.
  • CCPA (California): We respect the privacy rights of California residents and provide options to opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information by adjusting device ad settings.

5. Age Restriction (Children's Privacy)

This app is intended for mature audiences due to satirical and absurd humor. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or 16 in certain EU countries). If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with data, contact us for immediate deletion.

6. Generated Content, Sharing and Friend Challenges (Not a Social Network)

We do not host a public feed, public profiles, or a social timeline. AI stories are generated on our servers and cached, so another user who enters the same idea may receive the same story, but not your identity.

Friend challenges: when you tap "Challenge a friend", we store your UID, a reference to the story, and your four choices, and create a link with a code. Anyone who has this link or code can open the challenge and see the story and your result. They do not receive your UID or your story idea. Share challenge links only with people you choose.

When you finish someone else's challenge, we store your four choices with your UID. The challenge creator sees your brain age and choice match, but not your name or UID. The app tells you this on the result screen.

Daily Lore: we store your first result for each daily story, linked to your UID, and show players combined statistics about the paths taken. These statistics do not identify you.

Cards and recordings: result cards and collectible cards are created on your device. The app accesses your photo library only to save a card when you choose to save it, and screen recordings are made by you using your device's own tools. You are solely responsible for any content you choose to share on third-party platforms.

7. Data Retention & User Rights

We retain generated stories, including the idea you entered (cached_stories), for up to 30 days to optimize performance, and friend challenges for up to 30 days, after which they are automatically deleted. Ad reward records, challenge results and Daily Lore results are kept for up to 30 days, then automatically deleted. Push tokens are kept for up to 90 days after your last update. Progress backups and recovery codes are kept for up to one year after your last save. Anonymous UIDs and their associated generation limits or ban statuses are retained permanently to enforce fair usage and maintain a safe environment. You have the right to request the deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

1. Які дані ми збираємо (Анонімні User ID)

Ми не збираємо ваші імена, пошти чи контакти. Ми використовуємо Анонімну Авторизацію Firebase, яка створює унікальний Анонімний User ID (UID). Ми не відстежуємо апаратні ідентифікатори вашого пристрою. Цей UID зберігається у нашій базі виключно для:

  • Контролю щоденних лімітів на генерацію.
  • Фіксації спроб згенерувати заборонений контент.
  • Вічного блокування (бану) акаунтів порушників для збереження безпеки.
  • Нарахування нагород за рекламу, роботи викликів другу та Лору дня (див. розділ 6).
  • Резервного копіювання карток, пакунків і серії.
  • Надсилання сповіщень, якщо ви їх дозволили.

Ваші ідеї для історій (текстові запити) зберігаються на наших серверах разом зі згенерованою історією (див. розділ 7). Будь ласка, не вказуйте в них особисті дані.

Резервна копія прогресу: ваша колекція карток, пакунки та серія Лору дня зберігаються на наших серверах і прив'язані до вашого UID. Код відновлення дає змогу перенести їх на інший пристрій. Ми зберігаємо лише хеш коду, він діє один раз, а кожен, хто має код, може забрати цей прогрес, тому тримайте його в таємниці.

Сповіщення: якщо ви дозволили сповіщення, ми зберігаємо токен сповіщень і вашу мову, прив'язані до UID. Сповіщення доставляють Apple Push Notification service і Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Зберігається лише на вашому пристрої: мова та налаштування застосунку.

Коли ви проходите чужий виклик, ми зберігаємо ваші чотири вибори разом із UID. Автор виклику бачить ваш вік мозку та збіг виборів, але не ваше ім'я чи UID. Застосунок попереджає про це на фінальному екрані.

Застосунок звертається до фототеки лише для збереження картки, коли ви самі цього хочете, а запис екрана ви робите засобами свого пристрою. Ви несете повну відповідальність за контент, яким вирішите поділитися на сторонніх платформах.
